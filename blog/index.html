  1. November 24, 2026

    Sisyphus: a fast circuit-level distance estimator for quantum codes

    This post introduces Sisyphus, an open-source estimator of circuit-level distance that finds light undetected faults in qLDPC circuits with over a hundred thousand fault mechanisms, often in seconds. It explains how the search works, compares it with existing tools, and shows how it serves as a fast filter in upcoming work on optimal-depth syndrome-extraction circuits for ultra-high-rate codes.

  2. June 2026 · on Sunny He’s blog

    Round-robin CCZ is all you need

    Z. He, V. Menon, T. Yoder

    A post on my friend Sunny He’s blog that we wrote together. Sunny is a graduate student at MIT and an expert on qLDPC surgery. The post explores how any code-space-preserving circuit of diagonal gates is generated by certain primitive “round-robin” gates, and applies this to finding transversal non-Clifford gates.
